# DriveReady Support

If you need help, include:

- your account email
- vehicle registration
- the screen you were using
- the time of the problem
- the error message you saw

## Privacy and data choices

DriveReady supports:

- data export
- account deletion
- reminder preference changes
- push-device removal

These controls are available from the app settings and support screens.

## Current feature scope

DriveReady currently supports:

- vehicle records and due dates
- document storage and replacement
- trip checks for supported charge-zone datasets
- petrol and diesel forecourt pricing
- EV charger discovery, with live tariffs only when a tariff provider is configured

Parking guidance is not live yet and is not promised as a current feature.
