# DriveReady Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-12

DriveReady helps drivers keep vehicle records organised, monitor due dates, and check trip and refuel information before travelling.

## Data we store

DriveReady may store:

- account profile details such as your name, email address, phone number, and address
- vehicle details you add, including registration plate, due dates, notes, and provider-enriched vehicle data
- uploaded document metadata and the private files you choose to store
- reminder preferences, permission state records, push device registrations, alerts, saved zones, trip checks, and refuel searches

## How we use data

We use your data to:

- sign you in and keep your session active
- show your vehicles, reminders, documents, zones, trip checks, and exports
- generate due-date reminders and send push notifications when enabled
- create secure temporary links for exported files and uploaded documents
- support account deletion and user data export requests

## Third-party services

Depending on your environment and configuration, DriveReady may send limited request data to:

- Supabase for authentication, database storage, and file storage
- Mapbox for destination and location search
- DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service for vehicle data enrichment
- DVSA MOT History for MOT data enrichment
- Expo push notifications for reminder delivery
- supported UK retailer fuel feeds and a configured EV tariff provider for refuel pricing

DriveReady does not intentionally sell personal data.

## Data retention

Your data is retained until you delete it, remove your account, or your organisation removes the deployment data from its configured storage systems.

## Your choices

Inside DriveReady you can:

- export your data
- delete your account
- manage reminder preferences
- remove registered push devices

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support route published with your deployment or the contact information shown inside the app.
